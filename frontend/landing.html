Built for students who think out loud

Typing can get in the way when you already know what you want to say. Claros keeps the worksheet, voice conversation, and answers in one flow, so you can reason aloud and let Claros capture answers when you're ready.

Claros guides you question by question. You speak first; answers are written only after you state and approve them.

You "My answer is 12 because I added the sides."
Claros "Got it. Ready to write that for question 2?"
You "Yes, write it down."

Supported worksheet formats

Claros works best with clear, readable assignments.

PDF worksheets

Upload standard PDF assignments. Typed or neatly scanned pages work best.

Numbered questions

Questions with clear numbering help Claros guide you step by step.

Readable text

High-contrast, legible text improves question extraction quality.

Privacy and trust

Simple, honest boundaries for a classroom tool.

Microphone for sessions

Claros uses your microphone during voice sessions. You control when a session starts and ends.

Answers stay in your browser

Your answers live in the app until you export. No account required to try Claros.

You approve every write

Claros does not silently fill in answers. Writing happens only after you state and approve.
