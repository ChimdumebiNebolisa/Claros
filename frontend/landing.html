Questions about Claros

Does Claros give students answers?

No. Claros is prompted to guide with questions and hints. The student still states and confirms the final answer.

Which PDFs work best?

Worksheets with selectable text and clearly numbered questions work best. Image-only scans may need OCR first.

When does the microphone turn on?

Only after the student starts a voice session and grants browser permission. Typed answers remain available.

Where are worksheets stored?

Uploaded PDFs and session state use the configured worksheet storage so the session can restore and export.
