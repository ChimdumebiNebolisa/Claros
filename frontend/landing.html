How a session works

One conversation, from blank worksheet to finished PDF. Claros guides your thinking; it never does the thinking for you.

  1. 1
    Upload your worksheet. Claros reads the PDF and turns each question into an editable field.
  2. 2
    Talk it through. Claros asks guiding questions out loud. It won't hand you the answer.
  3. 3
    Say your answer. "My answer for question 2 is 12." That unlocks writing for that question only.
  4. 4
    Export. Download the completed worksheet as a PDF, ready to hand in.

Writing answer for question 2…

The one rule: Claros never writes an answer you have not stated. Ask it to write first and it will say, "Tell me your answer, then I will write it."

Built around one rule

Everything in Claros exists to remove the typing barrier without removing the learning.

You approve every answer

Answer readiness is tracked per question. Claros writes into a field only after you have stated or approved the answer for that exact question. Stating question 1 never unlocks question 2. The worksheet shows an "Answer stated" badge when you are ready, and a clear "Writing…" state while text streams in.

Guidance, not answers

Claros teaches with clarifying questions, hints, and checks in a natural back-and-forth voice conversation you can interrupt at any time.

Worksheet-native

Your PDF becomes an editable worksheet. Every answer field stays yours: review, edit by hand, or clear anything Claros wrote.

Transcript and export

The whole conversation is transcribed live. When you're done, export a completed PDF by button, or just say "export PDF."

An accessibility tool first

Built for students with motor impairments, dyslexia, injuries, or anything else that makes typing the hard part.

Typing-free path

The full flow, from working through questions to exporting, can be driven by voice. Typing is always available, never required.

You stay in control

The microphone is live only during a session you start and end. Interrupt Claros mid-sentence just by speaking, or with the interrupt button.

Clear at a glance

High-contrast text, large type, visible focus states, and screen-reader-friendly live regions. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

Questions, answered

Does Claros give students the answers?

No. Claros guides with questions and hints, and it only writes an answer the student has already stated out loud. If you ask it to write before answering, it asks for your answer first.

What kinds of worksheets work best?

PDF worksheets with clearly numbered questions and a selectable text layer work best. Image-only scanned PDFs may need OCR before Claros can extract their questions.

Can answers be edited by hand?

Yes. Every answer field is directly editable at any time: before, during, or after a voice session.

When is the microphone on?

Only during an active voice session. You start the session, you end it, and the level meter shows exactly when Claros is hearing you.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app, upload a worksheet (or load the sample), and start talking. Uploaded PDFs and session state are stored in the configured worksheet storage so the session can restore and export; export downloads a completed PDF to your device.
