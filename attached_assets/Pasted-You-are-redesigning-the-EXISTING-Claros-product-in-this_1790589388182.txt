--------------------------------------------------

The previous redesign attempt made a major mistake:

It effectively rebranded Claros into a cream / botanical-green / oversized-serif / editorial lifestyle product.

Do NOT do that.

The redesign should feel like:

"What if the existing Claros had an exceptional product designer refine it for another month?"

NOT:

"What if Claros became an Awwwards editorial website?"

The current Claros BLUE identity should remain central.

Before designing anything:

- identify the existing blue colors from the codebase;
- identify the current supporting neutrals;
- identify the existing typography;
- identify recognizable Claros visual elements;
- document what should be preserved.

You may refine the exact blue shades, tonal system, contrast, typography, spacing and component execution.

You may introduce additional blues, navy, slate, white and very pale blue surfaces.

You may introduce ONE restrained secondary accent only if it solves a specific hierarchy or state problem.

Do not replace blue with green, beige, ochre, brown or another entirely different brand palette.

--------------------------------------------------
4. PRODUCT THESIS
--------------------------------------------------

Claros exists for students who find typing harder than thinking.

The fundamental interaction is:

worksheet
→ questions recognized
→ student chooses a question
→ student speaks or types
→ Claros helps when needed
→ student's words remain distinct from suggested wording
→ student reviews the EXACT final answer
→ student explicitly approves it
→ approved answer is placed into a completed copy of the worksheet
→ student exports that completed document

The student remains in control.

The worksheet remains the source of truth.

Claros is NOT:

- a generic AI chatbot;
- a tutoring dashboard;
- a PDF editor;
- an autonomous homework-answering bot;
- an enterprise workflow product.

The strongest product principle remains:

"The answer is yours. Getting it onto the page can be easier."

8. TYPOGRAPHY
--------------------------------------------------

Do NOT make giant editorial serif typography the entire visual identity.

If you use a serif:

- it should not dominate every heading;
- it should not make Claros resemble a magazine, museum, architecture studio, luxury brand or wellness company.

Workspace readability and usability come first.

10. IMPROVE THE REAL UX PROBLEMS
--------------------------------------------------

Focus redesign energy on actual weaknesses in the current product.

Pay particular attention to:

- competing hierarchy between conversation, answer editor and worksheet;
- too much unused workspace before conversation begins;
- narrow/cropped worksheet context;
- mobile vertical stacking;
- ensuring answer controls appear at the right moment;
- making voice status clear without dominating the experience;
- making typed input equally first-class;
- reducing repeated success/error/status copy;
- showing question progress without making a wizard/dashboard;
- making source context easy to inspect;
- making exact-answer review feel consequential;
- making the destination of the approved answer extremely clear;
- making answer placement visually satisfying;
- differentiating:
  - source worksheet
  - student's words
  - suggested wording
  - approved answer
  - completed derivative document

Do not solve these problems merely by adding more panels or cards.

11. CREATE THREE ACTUALLY DIFFERENT DIRECTIONS
--------------------------------------------------

Create THREE redesign directions in Design Canvas.

They must differ structurally, not merely in typography or decorative style.

All three MUST preserve the blue Claros identity.

All three should feel like the same brand.

Think of them as different product-layout hypotheses.

Possible structural axes to explore:

DIRECTION 1
Question-first focused workspace.

The current question dominates.
Conversation/input is immediately available.
Worksheet context appears intelligently when useful.

DIRECTION 2
Document-centered workspace.

The authentic worksheet remains strongly visible.
Tools attach contextually to the current question rather than living in permanent panels.

DIRECTION 3
Adaptive two-surface workspace.

Student work and worksheet context share space, but the relative emphasis changes according to the current state:
question → conversation → answer → review → placement.

These are starting hypotheses, not required final solutions.

Research before deciding.

13. MOTION
--------------------------------------------------

Motion should explain state.

Potential places to invest:

- microphone activation;
- transcription appearing;
- switching between words and suggested wording;
- moving between questions;
- entering exact review;
- answer approval;
- answer placement into the completed document.

Keep most other UI static.

Prefer subtle transform, opacity and spatial continuity.

Respect reduced-motion preferences.

No essential meaning may depend on animation.
