3. PRODUCT THESIS THAT MUST SURVIVE THE REDESIGN

Claros is not a generic AI chatbot.

It is not a dashboard.

It is not primarily a PDF editor.

It is not an "AI copilot" with glowing gradients and chat bubbles everywhere.

The core idea is:

Students who can think or speak their answer more easily than they can type it should be able to complete a worksheet through natural conversation while remaining in control of exactly what is written.

The fundamental product flow is:

worksheet
→ detect/organize questions
→ student speaks or types
→ Claros helps when needed
→ student reviews the exact proposed answer
→ student explicitly approves it
→ that exact answer is placed onto the completed worksheet
→ completed document can be exported

The student must always remain the author and final decision-maker.

The worksheet remains the source of truth.

The strongest existing product line is:

The answer is yours. Getting it onto the page can be easier.

Preserve that philosophy.

4. PRIMARY USERS

The main audience includes students who find sustained keyboard input difficult, including people affected by:

dysgraphia;

motor limitations;

temporary hand or wrist injuries;

pain or fatigue;

other barriers to typing.

However, Claros should not visually resemble assistive medical software.

It should feel like a strong, modern student product that happens to have accessibility deeply built into it.

Accessibility should be a first-class design quality, not a visual identity gimmick.

6. DO AN ACTUAL UX AUDIT

Before designing anything, identify what is currently strong and what is weak.

Evaluate:

Hierarchy

Is it immediately obvious what the student should do next?

Is the active worksheet question visually dominant enough?

Is the worksheet visible without competing with the task?

Are primary and secondary actions differentiated clearly?

Cognitive load

Are too many controls visible simultaneously?

Does anything resemble developer tooling rather than a student experience?

Are technical concepts leaking into the UI?

Are there unnecessary cards, panels, dividers, labels, pills or status indicators?

Conversation

Does it feel like a natural student interaction or another generic chat interface?

Are voice states understandable without becoming visually noisy?

Is the conversation subordinate to the actual worksheet task?

Exact-answer review

This is one of the most important states in the entire product.

Determine whether it truly feels consequential that:

this exact text is what will be written onto the worksheet.

The review step should communicate:

authorship;

provenance;

control;

destination;

confidence;

reversibility.

Worksheet relationship

The worksheet is not decoration.

The product should create a strong visual relationship between:

the question being answered;

the conversation;

the final answer;

the location where that answer appears.

Motion

Identify where motion would improve understanding.

Good examples:

answer moving visually toward its worksheet destination;

question transitions;

listening-state feedback;

successful worksheet placement.

Avoid motion that exists only to make the interface look "AI-powered."

Accessibility

Inspect:

keyboard navigation;

focus states;

touch target size;

visible labels;

reduced motion;

color contrast;

screen-reader semantics where possible;

typography;

mobile ergonomics;

voice-independent completion.

12. PRIORITIZE THE DEMO EXPERIENCE

Claros may be submitted to a product competition where judges could understand the app largely through a short demo.

The redesign should therefore make this sequence extremely compelling:

Student has a worksheet.

Student chooses a question.

Student speaks naturally.

Claros visibly understands the student's words.

Student optionally improves the wording.

Student sees exactly what will be written.

Student approves it.

The answer visibly appears on the worksheet.

Student receives the completed document.

This should feel like one coherent transformation rather than nine unrelated screens.

A judge should understand the value even with the audio muted.
