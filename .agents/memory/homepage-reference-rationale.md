Keep Claros's existing page structure and overall design. Make targeted refinements to its educational hero symbols and heading typography, not a wholesale redesign.

**Why:** The user superseded the broader homepage redesign: “stick with the design and structure that claros has now” and use the scattered symbols “on the actual hero of the page.” They explicitly confirmed that only this symbol treatment should transfer to the existing homepage.

The user then approved the symbols but said they were “too spread out” and that they did not like “the main font for this site.” Preserving the page structure does not mean preserving the original heading font.

The user's latest clarification supersedes centered clustering and text-avoidance: “scatter them like stars across a sky behind the text,” “spread them all over,” and “I don't care if it touches the text or not.”

**Why:** Repeated attempts to keep symbols clear of the text produced an unwanted circle or ring. The user explicitly permits overlap and wants some symbols noticeably farther away than others.

**How to apply:** Scatter across the full hero width and its interior, including behind headline and paragraph. Keep text and controls on a higher layer. Use uneven distances and asymmetric distribution, not a perimeter, circle, centered container, or text-exclusion zone. Preserve the rest of the homepage structure and content.

The user also wants “more symbols and spread them out more evenly.”

**Why:** The full-width scatter should have balanced coverage, not sparse patches or all the symbols grouped on one side.

**How to apply:** Keep irregular placement while balancing density across the entire hero, including its center behind text.
