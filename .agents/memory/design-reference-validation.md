---
name: Design reference validation
description: Avoid false research claims when using public Refero and Mobbin references.
---

Refero search-result snippets may index its persistent “Upload or drag an image” search control, which can make an unrelated product screen look like a file-upload example. Public flows may expose initial screenshots while later steps require login. Open the exact screenshot or flow and describe only the states actually visible; cross-check with public Mobbin flows or first-party product guides for behavior not visible in Refero.

**Why:** Multiple seemingly relevant search results turned out to be unrelated wallet, profile, or social screens when opened. The public voice flow exposed only initial screens rather than the full sequence.

**How to apply:** For any Claros design research, record the specific product, visible state, and supported interaction principle. Never infer a complete flow from a search snippet or gated preview.
