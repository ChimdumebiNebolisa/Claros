## Non-conflicts retained as invariants

The generations agree that the source is immutable; exact review and commit are
separate; typed operation survives voice failure; user/model text is rendered
as text; model output cannot own geometry; export creates a derivative; and
untrusted PDFs and provider output must fail safely. Those rules carry forward
without compatibility shims.
