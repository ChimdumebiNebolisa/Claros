## Gate escalation rule

A critical contract, security, accessibility, source-integrity, or exact-text
failure stops the gate. A high runtime vulnerability, missing audit, invalid
license, nondeterministic corpus result, failed browser/PDF evidence item, or
unresolved ownership overlap also blocks advancement. Lower-severity findings
must have an owner, target gate, and written rationale before the gate closes.
